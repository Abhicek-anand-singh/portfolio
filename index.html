Freelance • Political CampaigningGhumarwin, Himachal Pradesh

Grassroots Political Campaign, Ghumarwin

A freelance campaign-support project built on one idea: win through people, not just posters. Instead of relying only on rallies and social media, the strategy was to mobilise from the grassroots, starting with the village, panchayat and booth, and to listen to local leaders before shaping any message.

Approach

  • Ground mapping: panchayat, ward and booth-wise mapping of the constituency and its communities.
  • Mukhiya-level interviews: one-to-one conversations with mukhiyas, panchayat members, ward leaders, mahila mandals, yuva clubs and respected local voices.
  • Issue-based messaging: campaign themes drawn from what people actually raised, such as roads, water, jobs, farming, health and education.
  • Volunteer network: local booth and village volunteers trained for outreach, with clear roles and reporting.

Execution & my role

  • Door-to-door and chaupal meetings: direct contact with households and community discussions at village level.
  • Feedback loop: regular field reports on reach, response and concerns, used to adjust the plan.
  • Digital support: social media content, creatives and WhatsApp-based outreach to back up ground work.
  • Reporting: structured data in Excel / Google Sheets so the team could see progress area by area.
